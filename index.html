From discovery to decision

How to use a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet

A product directory helps you discover options. A useful shortlist records enough detail to compare the exact items you intend to buy.

01 / DISCOVER

Save the exact option

Search by product type, then note the listing name, source ID, color and size. Open CAFANS and locate that item. Check the available source link and shopping agent options rather than assuming every listing has the same ordering route.

02 / COMPARE

Measure before choosing

For clothing, compare chest width, body length and shoulders with a garment you wear. For shoes, distinguish foot length from insole length and check the seller’s size chart. Record measurements in consistent units alongside unanswered questions.

03 / REVIEW

Confirm before paying

If you use LoveGoBuy, confirm current purchasing rules, fees and supported items in your account. Where warehouse photos are available, check the received variant and visible condition before arranging international delivery. Keep the return deadline with your notes.

Useful answers

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product discovery list used to organize finds and source links for further research. This page provides a browser-based CAFANS catalog selection and comparison guidance. You do not need spreadsheet software to browse it.

Is this the official LoveGoBuy website?

No. This is an independent guide. Product discovery buttons on this page open CAFANS. LoveGoBuy account services, purchasing decisions and current platform terms must be checked with LoveGoBuy directly.

Where do these products and prices come from?

The eight displayed names, product images and indicative USD prices were taken from the CAFANS homepage catalog. They are a dated selection, not a live stock feed. Check the current listing and selected variant before making a decision.

Does “Buy it now” place an order?

No. The button opens CAFANS, where you can locate products and review their available details and ordering links. This page does not collect payments or submit orders.

Can every listed product be purchased through LoveGoBuy?

That has not been verified. Review the item’s current source link, available agent options and LoveGoBuy’s purchasing restrictions before adding it to an order.

Are shipping and taxes included in the displayed prices?

No. The displayed amounts are indicative item estimates. Domestic shipping, service fees, payment charges, international freight and applicable taxes may be additional. Use current quotes and the budget worksheet to compare your expected total.

How should I check QC photos?

Compare the received item with the selected color, size and specification. Look for visible damage, missing parts and relevant measurement photos. Ask about unclear details before the applicable return deadline. Photos alone cannot certify authenticity, composition or long-term performance.

What if I cannot find a product on CAFANS?

Search the exact product name or source ID shown on its card. A listing may have changed or become unavailable since this snapshot. Do not assume another listing with a similar image is the same item.
